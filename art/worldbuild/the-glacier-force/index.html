The Glacier Force

Nivora’s military and de facto government, who run the Snowbiter expeditions nobody else will take on.

ゴゴゴ

The Glacier Force is a group within Nivora and serves as the head of the nation. They are a group of Nivora's military might and they often go on Snowbiter expeditions to scout for when the next hunt it. They are constantly risking their life and everyone gets quickly replaced. They are barraged by the public for not doing good enough whilst having to deal with the fierce Snowbiters, taking up a task no one dares to do. Members of this group live a very difficult life.
