[[The Glacier Force]] is a group within [[Nivora]] and serves as the head of the nation. They are a group of [[Nivora| Nivora's]] military might and they often go on [[Snowbiters| Snowbiter]] expeditions to scout for when the next hunt it. They are constantly risking their life and everyone gets quickly replaced. They are barraged by the public for not doing good enough whilst having to deal with the fierce [[Snowbiters]], taking up a task no one dares to do. Members of this group live a very difficult life.
