**Runox** — Known as the Crimson Harborer, Runox is a hateful, revenge-driven Luxian man standing around six feet tall with dark curly hair resembling a modern mullet, whose entire existence has been shaped by rejection and a profound inability to belong anywhere. Born in the [[The Luxian Empire]], he was deemed a defective child by the holy prophets and discarded — tossed off the island and left to whatever fate [[All Father]] would decide for him. He survived, landing in a giant moss quarry in the [[The Virethorian Empire]] where he was discovered and raised by two Virethorian artisans who gave him a normal upbringing, yet Runox could never truly fit in. His Luxian physiology made him visibly different, and the constant belittling from those around him made him feel like neither a true Luxian nor a true Virethorian — he had the gifted body of a Luxian but couldn't live up to their high standards, and the isolationist culture of the Virethorian Empire kept him trapped and ignorant of his own origins. This identity crisis cultivated a deep-seated hatred and a singular obsession with combat as an outlet, with Runox ultimately caring about nothing more than the thrill of the fight and the pursuit of revenge against a world that discarded him. In battle, he wields the [[Power Core Codex#The Orbs of Crimson|Orbs of Crimson]] as his Power Core, condensing crimson energy into tiny, incredibly dense and destructive balls that he uses in versatile and adaptive ways — firing them like bullets from his fingertip in a finger gun motion for ranged combat, or crushing them in his palm to coat his hand in raw crimson for devastating close-quarters strikes. Uniquely, wherever Runox bleeds, he can generate a new crimson orb, making him more dangerous the longer a fight goes on. He is also capable of [[Attack Codex#Hyper Dash III||Hyperdash III]] and can occasionally push into [[Attack Codex#Hyper Dash IV||Hyperdash IV]] , making him a highly mobile and unpredictable fighter who adapts his crimson abilities to whatever the situation demands.
