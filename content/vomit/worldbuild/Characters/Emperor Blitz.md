**Emperor Blitz** — Known as the Thunder Berserker, Emperor Blitz is a [[The Luxian Empire|Luxian]] emperor, a member of the [[The Luxian Elite Force]] and one of the most physically imposing and authoritative figures in the world, commanding a heavy, suffocating presence that reflects his iron-fisted rule over his empire. He is an enormously bulky and robust figure, almost always encased in massive black metal-plated berserker armor lined with heavy insulated padding designed to retain heat, with reinforced metal plates on his palms specifically engineered for his signature combat technique. Unlike many fighters who develop their abilities by circumstance or hardship, Blitz grew up in royalty and forged himself through discipline — training relentlessly as a teenager to master his powers before entering military service as a young adult, where he proved to be a ruthless and devastating general capable of single-handedly decimating entire populations. His Power Core Ability, [[Power Core Codex#Han-Zen|Han-Zen]], gives him mastery over lightning, which he channels primarily through his palm strikes — driving his armored palm into a victim and then surging concentrated electricity through them, either stunning them in place or burning their skin if sustained. He can escalate this further into a massive thunder-strike, overloading the opponent with electricity and sending them flying. Defensively, his armor-clad strategy is brilliantly passive — he lets enemies strike his metal plating and uses Han-Zen to channel their impact back as an electric shock. On the rare occasions he removes his armor entirely, he sacrifices his defensive bulk in exchange for tremendous speed and the ability to use [[Attack Codex#Hyper Dash III|HyperdashIII]], and in select cases [[Attack Codex#Hyper Dash IV|HyperdashIV]], transforming from an immovable fortress into a terrifyingly fast and lethal combatant.
