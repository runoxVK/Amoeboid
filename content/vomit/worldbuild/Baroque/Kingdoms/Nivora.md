Classified as Mediocre and governed by a Stratocracy, Nivora is a poor, humble, and tightly fortified village nestled in the [[Sub-zero Summits]] region, defined above all else by two things: its punishing, bone-deep cold and the relentless threat of the [[Snowbiters]] that encircle it on all sides. The cold in Nivora is not merely uncomfortable — it is extreme enough that any liquid left exposed to the outside air freezes within seconds, and the village sits perpetually buried under thick layers of snow, giving it a bleak, isolated aesthetic of dark brown oak log buildings half-swallowed by white drifts, with tiny hills scattered across the landscape and paths that must be constantly cleared just to remain passable. Surrounding Nivora entirely are the Snowbiter bases — massive igloo-like structures where the creatures nest — and beyond those, enormous mountains that wall the village in and reinforce its sense of isolation and siege. Originally a tiny settlement that grew only modestly over time, Nivora has always been defined by its need to survive rather than to thrive, and its Stratocratic government reflects this — military necessity drives leadership, and a significant portion of its small, low-population community is made up of fighters, many of whom belong to  [[The Glacier Force]], the organization responsible for managing the constant Snowbiter threat through operations like the Snowbiter Hunt. The rest of the population consists of artisans, general folk, and refugees who have found their way to this remote and unforgiving place. Economically, Nivora is barely surviving — it is frequently robbed by other empires, has almost no meaningful trade, and relies almost entirely on locally produced food from its own farms and resources harvested from its own small mines just to keep its people fed and functioning, making it one of the most economically vulnerable locations in Baroque.
