Classified as Basic and governed by a Stratocracy, the Virethorian Empire is a militaristic, barbaric, and deeply nature-rooted empire situated in the [[Plagued Zone]], a lush, murky, and swamp-heavy region that has shaped every aspect of the empire's culture, architecture, and identity. What began as a small refugee camp on an island in the Plagued Zone slowly grew as more people arrived and the settlement became increasingly obsessed with defense and survival, eventually evolving into a full nation of hardened, barbaric fighters whose entire culture revolves around military strength and self-sufficiency. The geography of the Virethorian Empire is distinctly swampy and organic — mossy lakes, dense lush forests, thick tree roots sprawling across the terrain, and buildings constructed from the same dark swamp wood that dominates the landscape, giving the entire empire a wild, overgrown, and ancient feeling that stands in stark contrast to the sleek grandeur of places like the Luxian Empire. One of the empire's most defining achievements is its domestication and weaponization of the local [[Tarkan]], fearsome creatures that the Virethorians have learned to tame and deploy as biological vehicles in the form of Tarkan Jets, giving their military a unique and formidable edge. The empire's economy is relatively stable despite its isolation — it trades rare crops, unique vegetables, and Tarkans, and sits on substantial ore deposits that feed its weapons manufacturing, maintaining a reasonable level of resources through a combination of local production and selective trade. The empire's isolationist culture, however, keeps it deeply insular, and its deeply humid, tree-canopied climate — where dense foliage blocks out much of the sunlight — reinforces the sense of a civilization that has turned inward, building its identity around the dark, tangled, and untamed world it calls home.
