[[Chariot]] Backstory

Chariot's skin begins to tingle and burn. Not in a bad way though, in a good way. In a way that happens as a result of running near a fireplace from an insanely cold day outside. He enters his warm cabin after a long day of playing outside, relieved from the rush of heat that put his body back to normal temperature. It was one of the colder days in Nivora.

"Mommy, Mommy, [[The Glacier Force]] is coming back, they look so cool!" he shouts, grinning through chattering teeth.

His mother's eyes widen as the photon of fear enters her very pupils. Ten year old Chariot looks up to the Glacier Force, they are his heroes. To his mother, they were ghosts still walking.

She grabs her coat, "They must be coming in from their first reconnaissance mission, lets go see daddy!"

---

Chariot and his mother both leave their cabin and their cheeks immediately get bitten by the harsh cold. Chariot holds hit mother's hand, trailing behind through the rough snow. Each step comes with a satisfying crunching noise. A couple minutes of walking and they are both met with a huge line of people surrounding the path of the village entrance. Villagers already lined up around the road, leaving a narrow path for the returning soldiers. Some faces clung to hope, others sharpened with rage and some were devoid of emotion. Some were waiting for loved ones, some here for interviews, some were here for no reason at all. The Glacier Force is slowly revealed through the snowy fog. Soldiers pass the entrance gate slowly. They all march in a perfectly synchronized manner. As always there is not an ounce of soul in their faces. They look visibly blank . . . lifeless. Many corpses are carried by troops in these dark brown, wooden carriages. There is snow all over everyone, icicles growing off of beards, eyes are filles with anger, anguish and hopelessness. Chunks of flesh are missing from people's bodies. A man with his cheek missing from what appears to be a giant bite talks to himself in the carriage. He looks like he's going insane. His teeth and gums move up and down from the hole in his cheek as he recites what seems to be a prayer. There is not enough space in the carriages. Many soldiers with lost arms or other chunks of their body continue to march forward. Not everyone gets the luxury of bleeding out in a wooden box.

"You people are USELESS. All you do is go out and let more men DIE. What value did we derive from this reconnaissance, TELL ME," a villager shouts out.

"Another bloody shit fest, " says a fat bellied bearded man with what appears to be sauce stains on his shirt.

The chief doesn't even glance at him. Two soldiers in the back collapse. Three men -- one in the front of the line, his buddy next to him, and a man in a carriage spit out huge amounts of blood. The blood colors the snow red and drips from their chins. There is blood everywhere, on everyone. Chariot's mother closes her son's eyes as a carriage comes through with a barely alive man. His intestines and guts are littered all over the carriage and there was a slimy, pink tube coming out of his belly from what appears to be a giant scratch mark.

"What are THESE MEN DYING FOR!!!"

"WHERE IS MY LOVE, WHERE IS HE! BRIGARD, ARE YOU THERE!"

"THIS IS WHERE MY TAXES ARE GOING, YOU ARE ALL USELESS FIGHTERS!"

Tears roll off soldiers, villagers and kids. Those same tears freeze in midair, turning into mini cannon balls that crash on the red, bloodshot snow beneath. The soldiers continue marching forward, no glances, no emotions, solely with lifeless pupils.


This was not new for them, these soldiers risk their lives every week on these reconnaissance missions. Why? The [[Snowbiters|Snowbiter]] Hunt. Every year, these very intelligent, giant saber-tooth like, albino snow beasts, known as Snowbiters, prepare their pack for a hunt. The villagers of Nivora are the prey. It is unknown when in the year the Snowbiters will strike. The job of the Glacier Force is to find out when they will strike and alert the town and militia. They go out as a group into Frostbiter territory and check if the pack is out of hibernation. There are always Frostbiters guarding the hibernation spots as a defense and the Glacier Force has to deal with that every time. They come back  with significant casualties. The people are THE PREY. They will do anything in their power to survive. 

"DADDY!" Chariot finds his father in the squadron of troops, soul-less as usual, fists clenched and with obvious sorrow written on his face.

Chariot's mother stopped him from running to his father, "Good, he is safe. Let's go back home soon and meet him tonight in time for dinner. They need to report the status to the chairmen."

The only thing in Chariot's mind is how cool these men are. Risking their lives to keep the town alert. Brave soldiers. Skilled warriors. Fearless.

Suddenly, a quivering, scrawny villager stops the chief in his tracks, "H-H-HEY, my daughter is a h-h-huge fan of yours and I was wondering if you could give me an auto-aut-autograph for her." The chief stood tall, towering over the villager. He peered down at him with his one remaining eye, showing no signs of emotion or acknowledgment for that matter. A man in the back of the troop screams in anguish as blood spurts out of his thigh. Lost limbed soldiers pant of exhaustion. The chief had said a thousand words to this villager without a single vibration from his voice box. He shoved the villager to the side, not lightly, and continued the march.

"What asshats, these villagers don't appreciate or understand what these soldiers really do for us. Most of these people have never seen a Frostbiter. Ungrateful wretches. Lets go home Chariot," the two depart back to their cabin, leaving the still yelling, complaining and anguished crowd of people.

---

Many hours passed and Chariot's father still hasn't returned home.

"Chariot, listen to me. When you are older, I do not want you to join the Glacier Force, it is a death sentence. Focus on math, you love math done you? You should work on becoming a great mathematician and leave Nivora when you can to show the world what you can do. It is not safe here regardless," Chariot's mother says.

"I want to beat those [[Snowbiters]] to a pulp, I know I can do it. I'll be just like daddy," Chariot passionately says.

Chariot's mother tightly grabs his arms, "YOU LISTEN TO ME, DO NOT, YOU WILL UNDERSTAND WHEN YOU'RE OLDER! YOU HAVE TO LISTEN TO ME CHARIOT PLEASE BABY PLEASE!" His mother then breaks into tears very abruptly as she relinquishes control of his arm.

Just as Chariot goes to comfort her, a town announcement plays on the tall speakers.

"ATTENTION ALL CITIZENS, A SNOWBITER RAID IS IMMINENT. BARRICADE YOUR HOUSES. THE MILITARY WILL BE OUT SHORTLY. ENFORCE PROPER PROTOCOL. GRAB WEAPONS AND HIDE UNDERGROUND. I REPEAT, A SNOWBITER RAID IS IMMINENT! "

Silence fills the room abruptly. Many of the military men have power core abilities, though not very strong. The sounds of thousands of boots running and crunching the snow beneath can be heard, echoing through the town. Chariot and his mother have gone through this protocol lots of times. They know their father's life is at risk and they know what to do to stay secure. Chariot and his Mother rush to barricade the walls as much as possible. Planks, tables and re-enforced metal. Chariot's mother feels a chill down her spine

*Why is it so abrupt. Did the Glacier Force miss something? This can't be fucking happening.*

"THEY ARE HERE EARLY!" A soldier outside yells in agony as a squelching snap ends his life. The sounds of crunching bones and squishing can be heard A LOT.  The various different sounds of power core abilities fill the village. The sounds of screaming and painful yells. The sounds of fire from foreign imported [[Scorch Slime]] grenades. The sounds of arrows flying and icicle guns. All these sounds, all at once. It's chaos. 

**P A N I C** ensues

Loud banging can be heard on the walls and doors of Chariot and his Mother's cabin.

"LET USSS INNN LET USSS INN PLEA–", a squelching, crunching sound stops the pleading speech. Many of these men are facing a Frostbiter for the first time. They are completely paralyzed in fear. It is not uncommon for soldiers to cowardly hide, not do anything, panic or try to escape. A man, with a completely blank expression, hiding behind three wine barrels near the barracks, takes his gun, sticks it in his mouth, smiles, and fires, painting the snow with his brains. There are however some brave soldiers who put up a solid fight, typically those with Power Core abilities. The Frostbiters all charge in the town, seeping into the residential areas and devouring everyone. The beasts poured into Nivora like an avalanche of fangs and claws. This year, the hunt came abruptly, the town didn't have enough time to prepare. Chariot's mother continues covering him up as tears roll down her eyes. Screams, squeezing, cracking, squelching. An utter massacre happens outside as Chariot and his Mother sit in fear within the confines of the small, homey wooden cabin. It is completely dark inside minus the warm red light from the fireplace, Chariot and his Mother don't make a single sound. The security and isolation of the cabin is almost warm and re-assuring but at the same time there is still a vulnerable feeling lingering throughout; it's chilling. The frostbiters burst into the infrastructure of the village with a force that made walls crumble under the weight of their charge, their roars cutting through the blizzard like thunder. Even more soldiers rushed to meet them, spears braced, but the Frostbiters tore through the lines with a speed that mocked human strength; armor bent like tin beneath their jaws, shields splintered with a single swipe of claws rimed in frost. Panic kept spreading faster than fire—families fled into the snow, only to be dragged down screaming, choking on the snow and their cries muffled as the pack sunk their teeth into where they fell. Blood steamed against the white ground, staining the village in broad strokes of red.


More and more soldiers came knocking on the cabin.

"Don't worry Chariot, just be quiet ok? Daddy will kill them all," Chariot's mother attempts to comfort her son as she mentally crumbles and descends into madness.

The cabin continues to sit buried in snowdrifts like a jewel of warmth in a frozen, barbaric wilderness, its log walls glowing gold under the firelight that leaks through frosted windows. The barricading of it makes it feel even more secure. The two are untouchable and the warmth of the cabin hugs them.


More sounds are made at the door. This time, it is an erratic scratching sound with wood audibly being chipped off. That soldier must be in excruciating pain. A giant paw blasts through the door sending wood everywhere in the house. The second paw enters. In a blink of an eye the entire wall gets utterly demolished and a single Snowbiter dauntingly lays its eyes upon Chariot and his mother. Its gaze is gut wrenching. The white fur blows in the wind like dancing grass. So majestic yet so cruel The grey lining on the fur is stained with blood. Human eyes, guts and fat tissue can be seen stuck in its teeth. It grunts out and snarls as it approaches the two faster and faster. The warmth in the cabin suddenly disappears as the merciless cold of Nivora rushes in.


Chariot's mother glances at Chariot, seeing a sharp chip of wood lodged into his pupil. Blood drips out. Chariot's eyes are pierced with tears of crimson dripping at a faster and faster rate.

"GET AWAY FROM US!" Chariot's mother grabs her spear.

Both Chariot and his Mother brace, Chariot is too numb to even feel the pain of his pierced eye. He picks up his spear with nothing but pure hatred. He looks the Snowbiter in the eye.

"Chariot, stay behind me NOW!" Chariot's mother shields him.

Every step the Frostbiter takes shakes the cabin. Each step feels like it shakes the very fabric of reality for Chariot's Mother.

The Snowbiter lunges. Chariot's Mother launches the spear at the Snowbiter and the Snowbiter snaps it with his paws like a twig. One swipe is all it took. Chariot lunges his spear at the Snowbiter. A squelch is heard. His spear pierced the Snowbiter's neck. Chariot looks up with a small flame of hope. All color fades from his pupils as he sees the utter horror that towers above him. The Snowbiter's massive fangs are sunk in his mother's neck. The Biter crunches even harder and his mother's neck snaps, with an audible cracking squelching sound. The Snowbiter jerks up and rips the mother's head right off. Chariot agonizingly watches this unfold as his spear flies off the Snowbiter. With one final crunch, his Mother's head is devoured whole with one eyeball flying out, landing right on Chariot's foot. Chariot's entire stomach is filled to a brim with a sharp burning feeling and tears roll down his eyes, mixing with the blood coming from the gaping hole on his eye socket. His eyes sting, but not from the pain of the wood. Chariot rushes at the Snowbiter with his mere fists, tears skewing his vision. The Snowbiter inserts its fearsome claws right in Chariot's face, pinning him down to the floor. Its claws have just turned Chariot's other eye into meat stew, sinking deep within his other eye. Chariot screams in such agony, it sends shivers through the whole village; through all the commotion already happening. Chariot can no longer see anything. He keeps screaming and flailing. Darkness devoured him as he desperately continued punching and slapping the Snowbiters, thick, meaty and furry pinning arm out of desperation.

"W-W-WHY WON'T YOU JUST DIEEEEEE!"

Then–


A huge shake and thud is heard throughout the house from seemingly nothing. Next came an explosion; Not a normal explosion. The sound of ruffling and grappling, followed by a giant meat ball exploding. The cabin is painted in blood and guts. The smell of dead Snowbiter fills the room and Chariot lies still, petrified. The Snowbiter had just been completely mangled, torn apart and used as paint to cover the walls with red guts.

Chariot stands up, seemingly unphased.

---

Hiding soldiers look at the cabin entrance with curiosity. Walking out, comes a tall, extremely muscular, all matte black man. No face, no features at all, just a black man, covered in matte black armor reminiscent of a medieval knight. One couldn't even call it a man, more like an entity. This man had just single handedly eviscerated a Snowbiter within the cabin with its bare hands. The "man" sprints at an immensely fast speed, rushing out of the cabin. It targets every Snowbiter it sees. It draws a rapier and jabs one Snowbiter insanely fast, pushing the rapier in and out like 3 times a second leaving thousands of gushing holes on the Snowbiters torso. The Snowbiter collapses in agony. The "man" leans on his back leg, bracing for something. He pushes forward and dashes into a sprint with such immense power it cracks the ground behind him. He runs around the village slashing every single Snowbiter in sight. Heads coming clean off, bodies being cut in half and brains being pierced. As the man gets farther from Chariot, his armor slowly comes off until he is a bare naked "man". His figure appears to be extremely muscular. He uses his bare hands to impale the Snowbiters. He drives his knuckles right through the heads of more and more Snowbiters. He wrestles some, getting them in chokeholds, snapping their necks. Hours pass and with the combined efforts of this "man" and the soldiers, the Snowbiters have perished and the remaining have retreated.


Chariot's father runs to the cabin and is struck with complete terror looking at the mangled remains of his wife. Quite a few meters behind the remains, stands Chariot, completely emotionless, eyes gushing with red. In front of  Chariot is a black void on the ground, sort of like a hole.

"The BLACK MAN, H-H-HE CAME FROM THE CABIN!" A quivering soldier whimpers.
