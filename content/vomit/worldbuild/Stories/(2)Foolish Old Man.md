3/25/26
1940

"These animals . . . they don't take me seriously father. I train the same way as them, THE SAME exact way. Why can I not tame a [[Tarkan]]? The worse part is seeing all the other men tame theirs and I get left behind. I am behind and the worst part it, I frankly don't care to tame a Tarkan. I have no place in this society," the despondent man says as he stares into the intense masculine hunter eyes of his father.

"WHAT DO YOU EXPECT BOY! You have been given the blessing of the Luxian body and still cannot match your peers. On top of that, you DONT CARE?! What will you do with your life [[Runox]]," his father groans with his raspy voice.

"I DONT CARE BECAUSE I DONT WANT TO FALL INTO THIS LOOP THAT ALL OF YOU FALL INTO! Is this not a boring way to live? Do you not find this all meaningless. There is an entire world out there and we as a empire sit cradled in our little island. WHY DO WE EVEN TAME THE TARKAN IF WE DON'T FLY OUT!? [[The Virethorian Empire|Virethorian]] life is SUFFOCATING," Runox swallows the unrivaled truth that he has a natural edge over the rest of the empire, yet he cannot climb the same as them.

"It is YOU who will suffocate yourself in your inability to motivate yourself boy. You will DROWN in inaction. You have the potential to be the most ruthless soldier in this empire with your gifted body. You simply do not use your benefit and that is out of your own whim. And why is it you say?"

"Its because I do not care to father, why must I."

"SO then what do you care about," his father focuses his pupils on Runox's.

Runox's face lost all sense of life and grumbles, "I want to annihilate the cult of [[All Father]] in [[The Luxian Empire]]."

Runox had thought about leaving, however he is burdened by the fact that these people raised him. He feels an obligation to owe them back.

"Childish FANTASIES. You cannot even beat your peers or tame a Tarkan, what makes you think YOU ARE SURVIVING ONE SECOND IN THE LUXIAN EMPIRE! YOU have not even discovered your Power Core," his father leans his weight on the table, sinking it into the wooden floor. "Boy . . . the truth is, I have no will to exact on you, I just want to see you become ruthless. I want you to be so free you can move the Earth."

Runox hesitates from that statement. He is conflicted. In an immature attempt to stay defensive, "I deserve to see my homeland!" Runox exclaims.

"We will not be taking you to the Hell Above, that is [[The Luxian Empire]]," his father closes the conversation. "You . . . are hopeless boy, leave my site."

---

Runox leaves the room with boiling confliction he has never touched before. Dawn approaches and the Vierthorian Guards come out for their defense shifts. The Tarkan Hangar seems to have lost many guards.


Runox ponders under a marsh, dilapidated ancient tree near the swamp in which he fell in from when the Luxians cast him out. A seed is planted in Runox's head. He takes a peek at the Tarkan Hangar.


*I can do both. I can pursue my will and satisfy my mother and father. I just need to take out the cult of [[All Father]].*


Runox walks to the Tarkan Hangar gates with a cold gaze. He is a whisper in the night and each step emanates relentless power. The Virethorian Guards turn their eyes to the approaching terror in the night. They ready their spears and their thorn spiked armor and helmet oppose any force. Runox continues to approach.


One guard gives Runox the gaze of death. He is not respected in this empire as Luxian. The guard will pierce right through him given even little justification.

---

The first guard thrusts his spear at Runox's head, unleashing a barbaric cry. Runox pivots to the left while grabbing his spear with his left hand. He pulls the spear with immense force, closing the distance. He then puts all of his strength in his right arm, throwing a devastating punch through the thorns of the armor straight into the guard's nose. The force cracks the helmet and Runox's knuckles bleed due to the thorns. The guard collapses to the floor as the second guard lunges forward. Runox's hand seems to be covered in gushing blood, however something is peculiar. The blood seems to have been hardened into some hard, dark red mass.


Runox gets tackled by the second guard and his thorns pierce all of Runox. Runox braces the fall with his right arm. The hardened blood cracks and releases a devastating explosion around the hand, sending Runox's elbow flying towards the guard's face at an insanely fast speed. His skull gets utterly crushed. The third guard with the horn flinches due to the blast and looks at it. He looks down.

"WHAT THE FUCK WHERE DID HE GO"

Runox is gone. The guard readies his horn again, this time faster. Runox swipes at the guard's horn from behind and it falls. At this point a lot of the neighboring villages are alerted. The guard and Runox engage in hand to hand combat. Runox throws a right cross and it gets parried. Lots of different punches and combos are thrown and parries and weaves are exchanged. Runox dashes in and uses the side of his body to slam the guard against the wall. He then follows up with a flurry of chops on the guard against the wall. The guard finds an opening and grabs Runox's neck fast, slamming him to the ground. Runox squeezes his arm. The guard grins with all his teeth as he squeezes harder, sinking the thorns into Runox's neck.


Just then, Runox remembered what happened when the hardened blood cracked. He saw more of it on the guard's hand from the choking. He smashed the hardened blood with all his might and it blew up the guard's hand entirely. Blood gushes out as he lets out a painful cry.


Runox gets up hastily and sweeps his leg. As the guard falls Runox uses all of his might to drive the guard's head into the ground as hard as possible with the fall.


Runox needs to be as quick as possible. He punches the Hangar lock with his knuckles. The hardened blood cracks once again, leaving a localized explosion, breaking the lock.

"This is where it works," Runox is filled with unwavering determination.

---

He locates a Tarkan and quickly straps on high grade Tarkan Jets as well as a breathing mask. Runox sloppily connects all the metallic joints and screws. Despite his inexperience, he Frankensteined the gear on the Tarkan, ready for launch.


Runox hesitates and thinks of his mother. He then reminds himself that this is merely a means to prove his natural benefit is not given to him in vain, and that he will be returning.


Runox guides the Tarkan outside and is met with a horde of angry villagers. He clicks on the Tarkan Jets and lets the Tarkan completely loose.


The Tarkan roars and it follows the thrust of the jet, launching off of the land immediately. Runox does his best to keep the Tarkan on track — it is much easier to handle a Tarkan with all this gear.

"Why do they make us tame Tarkans without anything when we live in an era of this technology. With this gear, there is no need to tame a Tarkan by hand," Runox relishes in his achievement.

---

The air pulls Runox's skin back as he continues to soar. Suddenly, flying spears emerge from below. The Virethorian guards are trying to take him down. Runox steers to the left, then to the right. The Tarkan's wings flap, Runox pulls up and steers to the left again. He cranks the engine — the whirring of the thruster gets louder. He can feel the fire. The Tarkan puffs and the mechanical whirring of the breathing mask activates each time. The sky is filled with mechanical whirring, spear heads, and the adrenaline filled breathing of Runox himself. The Tarkan continues to fly up and out of the island.


The Tarkan squeals. A spearhead has pierced its left wing.


Suddenly, Runox hears electricity mixed with a heavy fire. The left Tarkan Jet is pierced and down. Runox cracks the engine up even more to get out. The Tarkan flaps its wings with panic. The two are going so fast that Runox can barely see, even with his strong Luxian skin. His face is being utterly peeled and the wind cuts through.


The Tarkan puffs and puffs. No mechanical whirring. Runox's eyes widen — the breathing mask fell off.

"FUCK FUCK FUCK, I FUCKED UP PUTTING THE STRAP O——"

Runox gets washed with a freshness so great he almost faints. The air is so cool, so gentle. The stuffiness — it's gone. He can see clearer than ever. This is what it's like to fly weightless, he thinks to himself. It feels like finally entering the cold air after being trapped in a sauna for months. This is what it feels like to SEE. To BREATHE. To Live. Runox has exited [[Plagued Zone]].


Runox looks down. "The green smog — it's BELOW ME!" He is in utter awe. He relishes in the experience. *Now then, I will stop at a nearby island and find a means to the Luxian Empire after that. It's so annoying how we have no maps in [[The Virethorian Empire]]. I have no idea where the Luxian Empire is, I could benefit from talking to someo——*

"Wait a second, why is it getting hotter and hotter?" The burning sensation increases. Runox smells fuel in the air.


The Tarkan is on fire. Runox cranked the engine too much. It begins to squeal and vigorously flap its wings. The Tarkan begins to fall — it cannot keep its weight up due to the lack of plagued air.

"NOT LIKE THIS, I'LL GET TO AN ISLAND!" Runox cranks the engine to the max.

The Tarkan continues to soar. A lifeless ragdoll blasting through the air. A jet engine strapped to a carcass. The Tarkan continues to burn. The smell of burnt flesh fills the air and the sounds of cracking fill Runox's ears. The sound of the thrust shakes the very air around him. Runox continues to soar farther and farther away from the [[Plagued Zone]]. He goes higher and higher. Runox looks behind him and the Tarkan parts closest to the jet engine are pure bone. One third of the Tarkan is a skeleton now.

As Runox braced for a huge fall back down, he looks up with one last spark of hope. A single island emerged from the haziness of the sky. Runox desperately smothers his blood all over the Tarkan and watches it harden. He then gets into a squatting position with one hand holding the Tarkan's saddle handle. He then uses all his might to punch the hardened blood, jumping a the same time. The blast from the cracked blood sends him soaring upwards and the Tarkan explodes into gory chunks below. Runox's hand just barely grabs at the edge and he pulls himself up. The island reveals itself.

"I've done it!"
