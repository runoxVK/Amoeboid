## Hyper-dash
The Hyper dash is essentially a super dash that can be broken down into four tiers. It is typically used by users to close distances or make distance as quickly as possible. It is a fundamental skill that most fighters train to learn.
#### Hyper Dash I:
The most basic form. Involves a quick velocity burst, covering mediocre distance and leaving a tiny dust trail behind the user. It is pretty slow, like a normal human dash.
#### Hyper Dash II:
Still basic but a slightly better form. It also involves a quick velocity burst and can travel a LOT farther than [[Attack Codex#Hyper Dash I|Hyper Dash I]]. It usually cracks the ground where the user left, leaving a huge smoke trail. This is harnessed by a well trained fighter.
#### Hyper Dash III:
Much more advances than any of the predecessors and it is often used by very advanced fighters. The dash is almost instantaneous and devastatingly destroys the ground where the user left, leaving a giant sonic boom and dust. It can cover massive distances.
#### Hyper Dash IV:
The strongest form, often wielded by the strongest or a super charged state. The dash is instantaneous and can travel ridiculously far, leaving a giant sonic boom, destroying all the ground behind the user as the user dashes, leaving a trail of dust and rubble.
